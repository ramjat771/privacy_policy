Delete Account

रोजगार साथी: Jobs & Vacancy

You can permanently delete your account directly from the application. This action is irreversible.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the रोजगार साथी: Jobs & Vacancy app.
  2. Sign in using your registered mobile number.
  3. Go to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your request.
After confirmation, your account and associated personal data will be permanently removed.

Data Deleted

  • Profile Information
  • Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email (if provided)
  • Education Details
  • Skills
  • Profile Photo
  • Saved Preferences
  • Other account-related personal information

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required by applicable laws or for security and fraud prevention purposes.
