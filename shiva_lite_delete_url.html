Shiva Lite — Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users can request deletion of their Shiva Lite game account and any personal data that may be associated with the game.

Important: Shiva Lite does not require users to create a separate username/password account. If you use Google Play Games or another Google authentication service, that authentication is provided by Google and is separate from a Shiva Lite account.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you believe Shiva Lite has stored information associated with your gameplay or account and you want that information deleted, you can submit a deletion request using the contact method below.

  1. Send an email to the Shiva Lite support address shown below.
  2. Use the subject line: Shiva Lite - Data Deletion Request
  3. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant game record, such as your in-game name or player ID, if applicable. Do not send your Google password or other sensitive credentials.
  4. We will review the request and delete eligible data associated with the game, subject to applicable legal or security requirements.

What Data May Be Deleted?

Depending on the features you use, a deletion request may cover information stored by the Shiva Lite application or its backend, including:

  • Shiva Lite player/account identifiers, if stored.
  • In-game profile information stored by Shiva Lite.
  • Game progress or gameplay records stored on Shiva Lite servers.
  • Support information associated with your deletion request.
  • Other personal information that Shiva Lite directly stores and can reasonably associate with the player.
No Google password is collected: Shiva Lite does not ask users to provide their Google password to the game. Google authentication is handled through Google's authentication services.

Google Play Games / Google Account

If you use Google Play Games or Google Sign-In, the Google account and Google Play Games profile are managed by Google. Shiva Lite cannot delete your Google account or Google-managed Play Games profile.

Requests concerning your Google account or Google-managed services should be handled through your Google account settings and Google's own account/data controls.

Important: Uninstalling Shiva Lite from your device does not necessarily mean that information held by a third-party service such as Google has been deleted.

Deletion Processing

After receiving a valid request, we will review the request and take reasonable steps to delete eligible Shiva Lite data that we control.

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law, to prevent fraud or abuse, to resolve disputes, or for legitimate security purposes. Any retained information will be handled according to applicable privacy requirements.

Once the applicable retention period ends, retained information will be deleted or anonymized as appropriate.

What Happens When Data Is Deleted?

  • Eligible Shiva Lite account/game data will be removed from systems controlled by Shiva Lite.
  • Some game progress or leaderboard-related information may no longer be recoverable.
  • Deletion does not restore previously deleted information.
  • Data controlled by third-party services may be subject to those services' own deletion processes.
