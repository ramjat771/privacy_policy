Shop999 - Data Deletion Policy

Company: Prime 11 Sports Pvt. Ltd.

At Shop999, we respect your privacy and provide users with the option to request deletion of their account and personal data.

1. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you use Shop999, we may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Password
  • Location information

2. How to Request Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Shop999 account and associated personal data by contacting our support team through the contact details or support option provided in the app.

To process the request, we may ask for your registered phone number or other information necessary to verify account ownership.

3. What Data Will Be Deleted

Once your deletion request is verified, we will delete eligible personal information associated with your account, including account information and other personal data that is no longer required for providing our services.

4. Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required by applicable law, regulatory requirements, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business purposes.

5. Deletion Processing

We will review and process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period after verifying the identity of the account holder.

6. Account Deletion

After the deletion process is completed, the deleted account and eligible associated personal information will no longer be available for normal use through the Shop999 app.
