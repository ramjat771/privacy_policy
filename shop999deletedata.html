Shop999 - Data Deletion Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026
App Name: Shop999
Company: Prime 11 Sports Pvt. Ltd.

At Shop999, we respect your privacy and provide users with the ability to request deletion of their account and eligible personal data.

1. Personal Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Shop999, we may collect and process information such as:

  • Name
  • Registered phone number
  • Account authentication information, such as password credentials
  • Location information, where required for app functionality
  • Other information that you voluntarily provide while using the service

2. How to Request Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Shop999 account and associated personal data by contacting our support team through the support or contact option available in the Shop999 application.

To request deletion:
  1. Contact Shop999 support through the available support/contact option.
  2. Provide your registered phone number or other account details required for verification.
  3. Our team will verify the request and account ownership.
  4. After verification, eligible account data will be scheduled for deletion.

3. What Data Will Be Deleted

After your deletion request is verified and processed, we will delete eligible personal information associated with your account, which may include:

  • Account profile information
  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Location information associated with the account, where applicable
  • Other personal information that is no longer required for legitimate purposes

Authentication credentials will also be deleted or securely invalidated as part of account deletion, where applicable.

4. Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period when required or permitted by applicable law, regulatory requirements, accounting obligations, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Where retention is required, such information will be retained only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose.

5. Deletion Processing Time

We will review valid deletion requests after verifying account ownership and will process the request within a reasonable period.

The exact processing time may depend on the nature of the request, verification requirements, and any legal or regulatory obligations.

6. Effect of Account Deletion

Once account deletion has been completed, you may lose access to your Shop999 account and services associated with that account. Deleted account information cannot normally be recovered.

Important: Please make sure that you want to permanently delete your account before submitting a deletion request.
