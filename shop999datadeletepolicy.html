Shop999 - Data Deletion Policy

Company: Prime 11 Sports Pvt. Ltd.

At Shop999, we respect your privacy and provide users with the option to request deletion of their account and personal data.

1. Personal Data We Collect

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Account authentication information
  • Location information

2. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your Shop999 account and associated personal data, contact our support team through the support/contact option available in the Shop999 application.

You may be asked to provide your registered phone number or other information necessary to verify account ownership.

3. Data That Will Be Deleted

After verification, eligible personal information associated with your account will be deleted, including account information and other personal data that is no longer required.

4. Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where required or permitted by applicable law, regulatory requirements, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

5. Deletion Processing

Valid deletion requests will be reviewed and processed after verification of account ownership.

6. Account Deletion

Once deletion is completed, the deleted account and eligible personal information will no longer be available for normal use through the Shop999 application.
